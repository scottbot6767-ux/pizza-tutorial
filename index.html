Essential Gear & Ingredients

Stock your kitchen with the pizza-making fundamentals

Tools

Pizza Peel

Essential for transferring pizza in and out of the oven. Wood for launching, metal for turning.

Pizza Stone or Steel

Absorbs and radiates heat for a crispy bottom crust. Steel heats faster, stone retains more heat.

Large Cutting Board

For stretching dough and slicing your masterpiece.

Kitchen Scale

Precision is key for consistent dough. Weighing beats measuring cups every time.

Key Ingredients

00 Flour or Bread Flour

High protein content for that perfect chewy, airy crust. Caputo is the gold standard.

Semolina Flour

Dust your peel with this to prevent sticking. Adds a subtle crunch.

Instant Yeast

The magic that makes your dough rise. Fresh or instant both work great.

Fine Sea Salt

Enhances flavor and strengthens gluten. Don't skip it!

03

The Dough Decision

The soul of your pizza — make it or buy it?

Classic Pizza Dough Recipe

Makes 4 dough balls (12" pizzas)

Ingredients

  • 500g 00 flour or bread flour
  • 325g water (65% hydration)
  • 10g fine sea salt
  • 3g instant yeast
  • 10g olive oil (optional)

Method

  1. Mix: Combine flour, salt, and yeast. Add water and mix until shaggy dough forms.
  2. Knead: Work the dough for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic. It should pass the windowpane test.
  3. Bulk Rise: Cover and let rise at room temp for 2 hours, or until doubled.
  4. Divide: Cut into 4 equal pieces (~210g each). Form into tight balls.
  5. Cold Ferment: Place in airtight container and refrigerate for 1-3 days. This is where the magic happens.

Quality Store-Bought Options

No shame in the store-bought game. These are solid choices:

Local Pizzeria Dough

Many pizza shops sell their dough. Often the best store-bought option.

04

Prepping Your Dough

Timing is everything for the perfect crust

3 Days Before

Into the Fridge

After your dough balls are formed, they go into the refrigerator. This cold ferment develops complex flavors and makes the dough easier to stretch.

~11:00 AM

Take It Out

Remove dough from fridge about 6 hours before cooking. If you're cooking at 5pm, pull it out around 11am. This lets it come to room temperature and relax.

~4:30 PM

Preheat Oven

Start preheating your oven as high as it goes (usually 500-550°F for home ovens). You want that stone or steel screaming hot.

~5:00 PM

Pizza Time!

Your dough is relaxed, your oven is ripping hot. Time to stretch, top, and cook!

Pro Tip: Dough that's too cold will spring back when you stretch it. Dough that's too warm gets sticky and hard to handle. Room temp is the sweet spot.
05

Cooking Techniques

The final stretch — literally and figuratively

1

Max Heat, Always

Crank your oven as high as it goes. Pizza loves intense heat. Home ovens at 550°F, outdoor ovens at 800°F+.

2

Preheat Forever

At least 30-45 minutes of preheating. You're not just heating the air — you're heating that stone/steel through.

3

Less is More

Don't overload toppings. The dough can't support a mountain of cheese. Thin, even layers cook better.

4

Semolina on the Peel

Dust your peel generously with semolina. Give it a shake before launching — if it doesn't slide, add more.

5

Fresh Veggies = Later

Delicate toppings like fresh basil and arugula go on after cooking. They'll wilt and burn otherwise.

6

Rotate for Even Cooking

Halfway through, rotate your pizza 180°. Ovens have hot spots — this ensures even browning.
